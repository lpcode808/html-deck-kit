# Interview bank

How it works: the agent asks 3 to 5 of these at a time, the most useful
first, each with a recommended answer you can accept with one word ("recs",
"Q4: b"). Your answer is recorded under the question with
`[ANSWERED YYYY-MM-DD]`; settled facts move to `brief.md`. "Skip" or "later"
is a fine answer: it gets `[deferred YYYY-MM-DD: reason]` and comes back.

The agent adds new questions here as the talk takes shape.

## Round 1: before anything is built

- **Q1. What's the talk, in one breath?** Title, event, date, booked length.
- **Q2. Paste the abstract or session description, exactly as submitted.** It's fixed text; the deck has to keep its promises.
- **Q3. Who's in the room, and how comfortable are they with the topic?** And with technology in general?
- **Q4. In the room, online or both? Seated, or people wandering in?** Online changes "raise your hand" to "type in the chat," and drops pair work.
- **Q5. What's the one thing you want people to leave with?** If they remember a single sentence, which one?

## Round 2: shape

- **Q6. What will people *do* during the session?** Watch, try along on their own device, talk to a neighbor, build something? (Recommended: give a "watching counts" lane.)
- **Q7. Is there a demo? Fully live, or "here's one I made earlier" plus a short live pass?** (Recommended: pre-made plus a live pass, with a recording as backup.)
- **Q8. What real artifacts can you show?** Screenshots, photos, finished work, a quote with a real name. Real beats mockups.
- **Q9. What comes right before and after you on the program?** Sets the energy and how much warm-up you need.
- **Q10. What should the last ten seconds feel like? What's the closing line?**

## Round 3: look and feel (can wait until the outline exists)

- **Q11. What colors?** Hex codes, a brand guide, a website to sample from, or "decide later." (Recommended: two or three colors plus neutrals.)
- **Q12. What fonts?** One for titles, one for everything else, or "system fonts are fine." They get downloaded into `fonts/` so the deck works without wifi.
- **Q13. Any logo or mark that needs to appear, and where?**
- **Q14. Anything to avoid?** Colors, imagery, words, tone.
- **Q15. Open `effects.html` and press around. Any effects you liked, and for which slides?** (Recommended: one or two, where they make a point. Magic keys if you like a bit of theater.)

## Round 4: logistics (closer to the day)

- **Q16. Where do attendees get the links afterward?** One page, one QR, typed-out URL under it.
- **Q17. Whose laptop presents? Is there a clicker? Will there be wifi?**
- **Q18. Should this deck be public at a URL, or stay a file and a PDF?**
