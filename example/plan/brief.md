# Brief: the example deck

A filled-in brief, to show what `plan/brief.md` looks like once the
interview is done. The talk is the kit explaining itself.

## The basics

- **Title:** Your slides, as a web page your agent can edit
- **Event:** none; a demo deck that ships with the kit
- **Length:** about 10 minutes · no Q&A held back
- **Format:** read on your own, or shown on a screen
- **Room and screen:** any browser, 16:9

## The abstract (fixed text)

> See how an agent interviews you before it builds. Watch changes arrive as
> suggestions you can accept. Leave knowing what to check before the day.

## Audience

- **Who:** presenters thinking about trying HTML slides
- **What they already know:** slide apps; maybe a little about AI agents
- **Devices and accounts:** a browser; an agent that can edit files

## The point

- **The one thing they leave with:** the kit brings the structure; you bring the look and the voice
- **What they do during the session:** watch; then copy the kit
- **The last ten seconds should feel like:** "I could do this"
- **Where the takeaways live afterward:** the repository (QR on Thanks)

## Look and feel

- **Colors:** indigo ink, warm paper, burnt orange accent (made up for the example)
- **Fonts:** system fonts
- **Logo or marks:** none

## Locked decisions

- Real screenshots of the kit, not mockups.
- The Live Timer stays: it's the one slide that shows code running.
